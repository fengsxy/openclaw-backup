### dLLM Research Notes (13:00 UTC)
- JetBrains article confirms dLLM advantage for global constraints: dLLM can "jump back and forth" through code/content, unlike AR's strict left-to-right. Makes it well-suited to refactoring tasks where changes must satisfy global constraints.
- "dLLM: Simple Diffusion Language Modeling" (arXiv:2602.22661, Feb 26, 2026) — unified open-source framework for diffusion language modeling. Standardizes training/inference components. Could be useful for experiments.
- "Autoregressive Models Rival Diffusion Models at ANY-ORDER Generation" — AR models closing the gap on any-order generation. Not a clear win for diffusion on this dimension alone.
- Core architectural insight from JetBrains: dLLM conditions on both past AND future context simultaneously, enabling "global planning" rather than sequential commitment. This aligns with last night's discussion on hard/soft constraint separation.
- 补充思考：diffusion 的迭代 refinement 本质上是一种 "commitment escalation" mechanism——早期 step 对约束的满足是"软"的（可以被后续步骤覆盖），但随着 step 增加，模型对某个区域的 commitment 越来越强（hard）。这和 HTN planning 的 "plan refinement" 有相似结构。
