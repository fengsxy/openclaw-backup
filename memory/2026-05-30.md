## Paper Review (Daily)

### 1. Diffusion Models Optimal for Low-Dim Multi-Modal (ICML 2026)
- 2605.30153 | stat.ML | Jingda Wu et al.
- Diffusion models achieve near-optimal O~ convergence for learning on union of low-dim subspaces
- Rate depends only on intrinsic dim k, not ambient dim (curse-of-dim removed)
- No bounded-density, smoothness, or log-concavity assumptions needed
- Multi-modal compatible — strong theory for what empirical diffusion already shows

### 2. CROP — Conformal Certification of Reasoning Trace Prefixes
- 2605.30085 | cs.AI | Matt Cheung et al.
- Conformal prediction for certifying valid prefixes in LLM reasoning chains
- Selects calibrated threshold, returns longest contiguous prefix with no annotated error
- Rigorous marginal coverage guarantee under exchangeability
- Verifier-agnostic; 6 process-supervised reasoning datasets tested
- Practical bridge between process supervision and repair

### 3. Entropy-Cut MH — Sampling for Reasoning Without RL Training
- 2605.30327 | cs.LG | Anay Mehrotra et al.
- Next-token entropy as proxy for decision points in LLM reasoning traces
- Entropy-Cut Metropolis-Hastings: cuts at high-entropy positions vs uniform random
- Mixing time scales with # of decisions, not # of tokens (theoretically proved)
- Outperforms RL-trained models on MATH500, HumanEval, GPQA Diamond, AIME26
- Without training, curated datasets, or verifiers

### 4. Heterogeneous Treatment Effect via Matrix Completion
- 2605.30319 | stat.ML | Anay Mehrotra et al.
- Panel data (n units × m times), unknown non-uniform treatment assignment
- Row-wise ℓ_2 bound: O~(sqrt(1/n + n/m²))
- First sharp row-wise ℓ_2 perturbation bound for low-rank approximation
- No knowledge of propensities needed; standard low-rank + regularity assumptions

### 5. Wasserstein Contraction of CAVI
- 2605.30253 | stat.ML | Adrien Corenflos et al.
- Coordinate Ascent Variational Inference (CAVI): contraction in Wasserstein distance
- Requires: transport-info inequality at fixed points + functional smoothness
- General, sharp; local convergence; general smooth manifolds; non-smooth spaces
- Applications: Bayesian Gaussian mixtures, probit/logistic regression with Pólya-Gamma

### 6. Joint Model & Data Sparsification via Marginal Likelihood
- 2605.29908 | stat.ML | Alexander Timans (ICML 2026)
- Joint ARD: simultaneously pruning features AND samples
- Symmetric model+data sparsification; preserves conjugacy; closed-form updates
- Robust to outliers/misspecified noise (extends SBL beyond homoscedastic assumption)
- Single Bayesian objective combining robust regression + influence function perspectives
