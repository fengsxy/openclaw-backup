## Daily Paper Review (FoCore)

**Paper**: Focus on the Core: Empowering Diffusion Large Language Models by Self-Contrast
**Authors**: Jinyuan Feng et al. (中科院自动化所 + 小红书)
**arXiv**: 2605.01373 | **Date**: 2026-05-02

**Core contribution**: FoCore — training-free DLM decoding strategy that:
1. Identifies HD (high-information-density) tokens via self-contrast
2. Temporarily remasks converged HD tokens as negative samples to steer generation
3. FoCore_A variant uses early convergence of HD tokens for 2.07× speedup

**Key insight**: HD tokens serve as logical anchors in reasoning trajectories; they converge earlier than surrounding tokens, enabling both quality gains and parallel acceleration.

**Research line connection**: FoCore complements VSB (commit decision), SWD (KL=MI bound), and Temporal Emergence (content vs function convergence). HD token dependency modeling is the most promising follow-up.
